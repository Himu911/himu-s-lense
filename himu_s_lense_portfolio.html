About Me

Engineering the Future

As a student of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE), my passion lies in the intricate world of microcontrollers and circuit simulations. I thrive on bringing complex automated systems to life, from smart home IoT devices utilizing ESP32, to precise industrial automation using advanced circuit designs in Proteus and LTspice.

My academic journey is fueled by a relentless curiosity to understand how things work at the elemental level, allowing me to innovate and build sustainable electronic solutions.
